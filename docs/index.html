Health Check-In

A private, opt-in daily check-in by text message that helps one family stay connected with their aging parents.

What this service is

Health Check-In sends a short, friendly sequence of text messages each morning to a small number of family members who have asked to receive them. Their replies help the family stay in touch and notice early if someone hasn't checked in. It works entirely over ordinary SMS, so no app or internet is needed.

This is a family information service, not a medical device. It records everyday observations and highlights trends to support family conversations. It does not diagnose conditions, detect emergencies, or provide medical advice.

Who receives messages, and how they opt in

Messages go only to a handful of consenting family members. Each recipient is enrolled by the account owner after giving their express prior consent in person to receive these daily texts. This is a closed, personal program: there is no public sign-up, no marketing, and phone numbers are never bought, sold, shared, or added without the person's direct agreement. Any recipient can stop the messages at any time (see Opt-out & help below).

What the messages look like

The check-in asks a few gentle questions, one at a time, and closes with a thank-you:

Morning check-in
Good morning! ☀️ How are you feeling today, on a scale of 1 to 5? (5 = great, 1 = rough)
Follow-up
Thanks! Have you taken your morning meds yet? (yes / no)
Follow-up
Got it. And have you had something to eat? (yes / no)
Closing
That's everything — thank you! 💛 So good to hear from you. Talk tomorrow.

How often messages are sent

Recurring, roughly one short conversation each morning (a few messages per recipient per day), plus the recipient's own replies. Message frequency varies.

Opt-out & help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy

Information shared through this service is used only to keep participating family members informed. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and mobile numbers and message content are not used for marketing. No consumer information collected through the messaging program is shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
